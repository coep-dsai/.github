## 👋 About Us

We are the **Data Science & Artificial Intelligence Club of COEP Technological University, Pune** — a student community that promotes research, education and hands-on projects in **AI, Data Science, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, NLP, Remote Sensing and GIS**.

Our club brings together students from **every engineering branch**, united by one thing: a passion for turning data into intelligent systems. We run workshops, hackathons, expert talks and build open-source projects that go beyond the classroom.

> 💡 **Our vision:** make COEP a haven for Data Science and AI, where learning happens by building, and collaboration happens across clubs, companies and institutes.

## 📅 Events & Milestones

| Year | Highlight |
|---|---|
| **2018** | Club founded; Python and ML fundamentals sessions for COEP students |
| **2019** | Hands-on deep learning session on Intel's OpenVINO toolkit |
| **2020** | **Predict-X**, an online data science hackathon with MindSpark; DSAI Live Sessions on computer vision |
| **2023** | **Neurohack 2.0**, a Generative AI hackathon with MindSpark |
| **2024** | Python workshop (50+ attendees) and an LLM guest lecture by a COEP alumnus |
| **2026-27** | New website, agentic AI and LLM automation projects, and hosting MindSpark Neural Nexus |
